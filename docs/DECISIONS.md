- 2026-10-26 Presto pilot sample: the first 10 `malawi_2023` rows in audit-file order (all walked polygons, tile 36LWL). Their fixed cohort window, 1 Oct 2022 – 30 Jun 2023, is passed to `download_sentinel2.py` as `--date-overrides` (`configs/sentinel2_windows_malawi_2023_pilot.csv`); the downloader's own 180-days-before-crop-cut window is not used because windows are fixed per zone (2026-10-25).
- 2026-10-26 Reflectance scale: `reflectance.tif` is unitless BOA reflectance = DN × 0.0001 − 0.1, the STAC scale and offset applied once by the downloader (the −1000 DN offset exists from processing baseline 04.00, so in every Collection 1 product). Presto divides Sentinel-2 DN by 10 000 and was pretrained on 2020–21 data without that offset, so the value Presto sees is our reflectance unchanged; the offset is never applied a second time (`cropyield.features.presto_inputs`). Offset-corrected values can be slightly negative over dark targets; they are passed through unclipped (none in the pilot's 55 scenes: minimum 0.0135 over valid field pixels, 0.0056 over all valid chip pixels).
- 2026-10-26 Known domain shift, accepted for now: Presto's Sentinel-2 pretraining data came from Earth Engine `COPERNICUS/S2` (Level-1C top-of-atmosphere reflectance, via openmapflow 0.2.2 pinned by the Presto repo); our chips are Level-2A bottom-of-atmosphere. Same numeric scale, different quantity (BOA is lower in the visible bands and higher vegetation indices are expected). Presto's pretraining timesteps are also 30-day per-pixel cloud-free mosaics, whereas ours are calendar months with one clear scene each. Fine-tuning is expected to absorb it; the frozen-embedding baseline should be read with this in mind. Switching to L1C was not chosen because the project's imagery decision (2026-10-24) is L2A.
- 2026-10-26 Presto band order: 17 NORMED_BANDS = VV, VH, B2, B3, B4, B5, B6, B7, B8, B8A, B11, B12, temperature_2m, total_precipitation, elevation, slope, NDVI. Our B02…B12 fill B2…B12; B1, B9 and B10 are not used by Presto; NDVI is computed from the normalised B8 and B4 exactly as Presto does.
- 2026-10-26 Masks: we have Sentinel-2 only. Sentinel-1, ERA5 and SRTM slots are 0 and masked at every timestep; Dynamic World is 9 (unknown) so Presto ignores it. A month with no accepted scene or no valid field pixel is masked for every band, so Presto drops that month's tokens. Cloudy pixels never enter: a month's value is the per-band median over field ∩ valid (SCL 4/5/6, all bands finite) pixels of the one accepted scene per month. Presto's own evaluation code averages a patch spatially (`presto/eval/eval.py`); the median is a deliberate robustness choice against residual cloud and shadow, to be compared against the mean later.
- 2026-10-26 Point vs polygon: polygon records use the pixels whose centres fall inside the field polygon, rasterised from `record.json` on the chip grid (`Chip.aoi_interior`); the downloader's `aoi_mask.tif` is rasterised with `all_touched` and adds boundary pixels (20–38 % of the pilot fields' pixels), so it is used only when no pixel centre is inside. Point records use the single 10 m pixel the downloader marks. A 3×3 neighbourhood for points is a later decision (ROADMAP).
- 2026-10-26 Presto code and weights pinned at nasaharvest/presto commit 11e207a668a34336ced1d8e492a1bd5849b96c4a: `single_file_presto.py` vendored byte-identical as `src/cropyield/models/presto_single_file.py` (MIT; SHA-256 fb487a3a9ceb6040eaa8e97b9cd2dd9df8cb6c650ab889ac0868944ed55700df); `data/default_model.pt` from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nasaharvest/presto/11e207a668a34336ced1d8e492a1bd5849b96c4a/data/default_model.pt (SHA-256 559c9d76f525bdf2dd3774da8b1e7f1510769b73baacac12e9e4d8d5c67fbe65, 3,321,716 bytes) fetched by `scripts/get_presto.py` into git-ignored `data/models/presto/default_model.pt` and verified on every load; `src/cropyield/models/presto_weights.py` is the authoritative record.
