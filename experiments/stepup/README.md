# Step-up ladder: where does the R² come from?

Generated by `stepup.py` and `report.py`. Same fields and `s1_cc2km` folds as step 8: fold-0 fields train, folds 1-4 are scored. Paired cluster bootstrap against A1 (Ridge on the 10 season indices), 2,000 draws, seed 20261029; `p Holm` corrects the rank p-value for every row of the cohort except R1. **MAE** is the mean absolute error and **Bias** the mean of prediction minus yield, both in t/ha. **R² between** = how well the cluster (village) means are predicted; **R² within** = how well the deviations from the village mean are predicted; **train R²** minus R² shows overfitting. The pairwise rank model predicts order only.
